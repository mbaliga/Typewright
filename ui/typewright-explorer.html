Each layer has a colour and a line pattern. Aligned at cap height, the invisible differences become obvious: width, stroke, where the curves turn.

bowlThe closed, round part of a letter that encloses a counter. Here the whole lower body of the a.
counterThe white inside a bowl. Counter space and letter space are the same thing to the eye.
stemThe main vertical stroke. Its width, 44 units here, is the font's most important number.
terminalThe free end of a stroke. One style for the whole font: flat here, so every free end is flat.
