## Hero

**Draw letters. Get a font. Learn why it works.**

Typewright turns hand-made letterforms into a font, and teaches you what a reviewer looks for while you work.
Free for everyone. Nothing leaves your device.

## Three lines under the hero

1. **Your drawing stays yours.** An accepted letter is locked. Typewright never redraws it behind your back.
2. **It shows its numbers.** Judged by how published fonts are built, as a spread, not a single score. Where a check
   is our own rule of thumb, it says so.
3. **Plain files.** A project is a folder of UFO and JSON. It opens in FontForge, Glyphs and RoboFont.

## How it works

Print a sheet. Draw. Photograph. Trace and accept each letter. Draw to fix. Space and kern. Check. On the Linux
desktop version, build the TTF and write the Google Fonts repository folder.

## What is early

- The **Workbook** (a drawing course) is Early: lessons are still being written.
- **Devanagari, kana and Arabic Naskh** are Preview.
- The **web version** is a Preview: it cannot compile a font or run Google Fonts' own checks. It exports your
  project and the repository folder as a zip, and the desktop app builds it.
- On **Android**, building the font file and Google Fonts' own profile are not available in this release.

## Private by design

No account. No ads. No analytics. The Android app has no internet permission. Crash reports stay on your device
and are shown to you to copy if you choose. [Read the privacy policy.]

## Licence, in one paragraph

The app is fair source (FSL-1.1-ALv2): free to use, change and share for anything except selling a competing
product, and each release becomes Apache-2.0 two years after it is published. The engine is Apache-2.0. Fonts you
make are yours. The name and icon are not licensed (`TRADEMARKS.md`).
