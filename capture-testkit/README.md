# capture-testkit

The synthetic photographs the capture and trace tests are made from, and the Hyle Deco fixture they draw: a printed template sheet with glyphs drawn into its cells, seen by a pinhole camera at a tilt and roll, unevenly lit, blurred, noisy, compressed and tinted. It is test support only (engine-trace's tests and the golden path use it) and ships in no app.

Every photograph is synthetic and seeded; none is a photograph of paper. See `PhotoSpec` for what each knob models and `docs/CAPTURE.md` for what that does and does not prove.
